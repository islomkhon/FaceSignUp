## 1. Acceptance

These Terms govern use of FaceSignUp. By creating an account, the user confirms
that they have read and agree to these Terms.

## 2. Eligibility and guardian consent

Users must be legally able to accept these Terms. A parent or guardian must
accept them and provide any required face-data consent for children below the
applicable legal age.

## 3. Account registration

Users must choose a unique name or nickname, protect their password, and choose
one security question and answer. Accounts, passwords, and security answers
must not be shared.

## 4. Face data and biometric processing

By registering, the user agrees that FaceSignUp may create and store an
encrypted numerical face template and a small encrypted profile thumbnail for
authentication, same-person verification during a scan, and authorized
administrator account management. Only the encrypted thumbnail is retained;
other raw camera frames are processed for verification and deleted after use.
Profile thumbnails are available only to authenticated administrators.

The user may withdraw consent and delete the account, face template, recovery
answers, profile thumbnail, and active sessions through the account deletion
feature or official support process.

## 5. Authentication limitations

Face comparison, movement checks, screen-light checks, password verification,
and security-answer recovery checks are security signals. They do not guarantee
that the device, camera stream, or network is uncompromised. The current
implementation is not certified presentation-attack detection or certified
identity proofing.

## 6. User responsibilities

Users must use a private, adequately lit environment, avoid virtual cameras and
injected video, use a unique password for name-based sign-in, and choose and
keep confidential a security answer that others cannot easily discover.

## 7. Acceptable use

Unauthorized access, interference, security-control circumvention,
impersonation, and unlawful use are prohibited. The operator may suspend access
when necessary for security, users, or legal compliance.

## 8. Availability and changes

The service may be changed or suspended. Material Terms changes will be presented
through the service, and further use may require renewed acceptance.

## 9. Privacy and retention

Data is processed for registration, authentication, security, recovery, and
operations. Face templates and profile thumbnails are encrypted at rest;
passwords and security answers are one-way hashed. Retention is limited to
operational and legal requirements. A separate privacy notice may provide
additional local rights.
